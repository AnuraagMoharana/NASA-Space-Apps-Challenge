SECTION 02 // KNOWLEDGE BASE

Key physical insights and scientific milestones unlocked through automated deep space exploration.

01
CELESTIAL MECHANICS

Gravitational Assist Slingshots

By stealing a fraction of a planet's orbital angular momentum, probes like Voyager and Cassini accelerated to velocities unattainable with conventional chemical propellants alone—paving the way to the outer gas giants.
02
ASTROBIOLOGY & HYDROLOGY

Subsurface Ocean Worlds

Data from the Galileo and Cassini missions revealed liquid water oceans hiding beneath the icy crusts of Europa and Enceladus, shifting our search for extraterrestrial life away from Goldilocks planets toward icy moons.
03
HELIOPHYSICS

Heliospheric Boundary Dynamics

Crossing the heliopause into the interstellar medium, instruments recorded the dramatic drop in solar wind particles and the influx of galactic cosmic rays—defining the physical edge of our Sun's magnetic realm.
